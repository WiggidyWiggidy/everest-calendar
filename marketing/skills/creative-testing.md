# Skill: creative-testing (the ad-angle/creative engine)

The systematic engine for "test lots of ad angles, creatives, and messaging." Current 2026 Meta success
is creative-led: broad targeting + Advantage+, and creative *variety* is the lever. Used by
`meta-ads-expert` + `campaign-operator` + `customer-avatar` + `consumer-psychology`.

## Concept vs variation testing (never confuse them)
- **Concept test** (big question — does this angle resonate at all?): pit distinct angles/archetypes
  against each other — founder story vs problem-agitation vs UGC unboxing vs proof/authority.
- **Variation test** (small question — which version of a proven angle wins?): take the winning concept
  and vary ONE thing — first frame, hook line, caption, CTA, thumbnail.
- Diagnose which you're running; the sample/time-to-significance differs (experiment-standards Rule 3).

## The hook × format grid (structured variety)
Grid = hooks × formats. Hooks: bold claim · relatable problem · social proof · founder-honest ·
allocation/scarcity · risk-reversal. Formats: UGC talking-head, static/carousel, demo, before/after.
Seed hooks from `voice-of-customer.md` + `customer-avatar.md` + `winning-hooks`.

## UGC-first (feed camouflage)
Short vertical 15–30s UGC consistently beats polished studio: it blends into the feed (delays scroll-skip)
and triggers peer-trust. Default new concepts to UGC-style unless data says otherwise.

## Budget rule (bootstrapped): 60 / 30 / 10
60% behind proven winners · 30% behind close variations of winners · 10% on genuinely new swings.
(For KRYO's tiny budget, scale the *ratio*, not the dollar count; keep test cells above the learning threshold.)

## Discipline (one variable, honest reads)
- 3–5 variations per ad set, each isolating ONE variable — else you can't attribute the result.
- Judge on the highest-funnel metric with adequate n (cost-per-ATC / CTR / hook rate), not purchases at
  low volume; purchase = guardrail. State time-to-significance (experiment-standards).
- Kill fast on leading indicators; graduate winners to the Scaling campaign.

## Evaluator-optimizer loop (make the creative smarter each round)
Generate → a *separate* critic scores each concept against a rubric (message-match to the ad's promise,
avatar-fit, objection addressed, hook strength, feed-camouflage, one-variable-clean) → revise → only then
propose. The generator never grades its own work (mirrors the launch playbook's no-self-certification).

## Output
Each concept ships as an Experiment Card (hypothesis, primary metric, sample, time-to-significance,
win/kill rule) via the launch playbook. Every angle names the avatar + objection it targets and the
ad→page pre-frame it must match.
