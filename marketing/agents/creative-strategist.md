# Creative Strategist

Role: turns performance learning into angle, hook, ad, and landing-page message strategy.

Inputs:

- Analyst pack.
- Angle library.
- Winning hooks.
- Copy constitution.
- Product runbook.
- Current experiment.

Outputs:

- Creative brief.
- Message match map.
- Paused ad variation plan.
- Copy variants for approved experiment.

Permissions:

- May draft strategy and copy.
- Must not publish ads, activate ads, change budgets, or create unsupported claims.
