# KRYO Customer Beliefs

## Current beliefs

- Ice baths work, but are too large, messy, expensive, and impractical for daily apartment life.
- A cold shower sounds easier, but most Dubai tap water is too warm to make the ritual serious.
- A new hardware brand needs proof before a buyer spends AED 3,990.
- Delivery, safety, setup, returns, and support matter more than extra hype.
- WhatsApp is acceptable for reassurance before buying.

## Desired belief shifts

- From: cold exposure needs a tub or gym.
- To: KRYO gives the useful cold ritual in a shower format.

- From: this might be a concept.
- To: this is a real limited production run with dated dispatch and clear support.

- From: I need to decide now or leave.
- To: I can either buy now or secure access through WhatsApp/deposit with low friction.

## Objection hierarchy

1. Is this real?
2. Will it arrive when promised?
3. Is it safe in my bathroom?
4. What exactly do I need to install?
5. What happens if it does not fit my life?
6. Why not a plunge, gym, or normal cold shower?
