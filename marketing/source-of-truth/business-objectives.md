# Business Objectives — Source of Truth

## Objective
Sell KRYO 2.0 direct to consumer in the UAE at a positive contribution margin.

## Current priority order (2026-07-31, evidence-led)
1. **Make mobile transact.** 80% of product-page traffic is mobile and converts at 0.36%
   vs 12.5% desktop. Nothing else changes the outcome while this holds.
2. **Establish trustworthy purchase measurement.** `shopify_orders` is empty; revenue is
   currently unmeasurable.
3. **Deploy WhatsApp lead capture.** The warm-buyer path is entirely unmeasured.
4. Landing-page and messaging optimisation — only after 1–3.

## Explicit non-goals
- Additional dashboards or reporting surface before the funnel can be measured.
- Creative-angle optimisation while `meta_ad_breakdowns_daily` is orphaned.
- Any B2B, manufacturing, CAD or supplier work — out of scope.

## Standing rule
Prefer one commercially meaningful learning over more dashboard complexity.
