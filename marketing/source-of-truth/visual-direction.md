# KRYO Visual Direction

Visual system should make the device feel real, premium, and apartment-compatible.

Priorities:

1. Real product presence.
2. Dubai bathroom context.
3. Water and cold mechanism clarity.
4. Low-friction setup proof.
5. Premium domestic calm.

Avoid:

- Generic ice images disconnected from the product.
- Overly clinical visuals.
- Impossible bathroom setups.
- AI-looking hands, fixtures, or water physics.
