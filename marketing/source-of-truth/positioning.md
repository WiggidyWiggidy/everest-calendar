# KRYO Positioning

KRYO is a semi-permanent Dubai-bathroom cold-shower appliance for affluent UAE apartment owners who want repeatable cold exposure without owning an ice bath.

Canonical product frame:

- Category enemy: bulky cold plunges, melting ice, gym ice-bath appointments, and high-friction recovery rituals.
- Main promise: cold exposure that fits a Dubai apartment routine.
- Mechanism: a controlled cold reservoir and shower output in a compact appliance format.
- Buyer: 35 to 54 year old Dubai male with disposable income, likely skeptical, auditing trust before spending AED 3,990.
- Primary objection: not whether cold exposure is interesting, but whether this specific company and device are real, safe, deliverable, and supportable.
