# KRYO Brand Voice

Voice: calm, specific, domestic, and proof-led.

Write for a skeptical Dubai buyer auditing a high-ticket appliance on an iPhone.

Use:

- Short sentences.
- Concrete nouns.
- Real dates and real quantities.
- Mechanical proof over adjectives.
- Founder-honest or team voice, but no named individual on-site unless Tom approves that scope.

Avoid:

- Hype.
- Biohacker language.
- Fake urgency.
- Medical claims.
- Em dashes in customer-facing copy.
- Any retired product names.
