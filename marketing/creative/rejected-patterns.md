# KRYO Rejected Patterns

Do not use these patterns unless a future experiment explicitly reverses this with evidence.

- Generic cold-exposure hype without product proof.
- Fake review volume or invented customer quotes.
- Medical or therapeutic promises.
- Vague scarcity such as "almost gone" without a real count.
- Making WhatsApp sound like support only. It should be access, concierge, or dispatch priority.
- Mixing buy-now, deposit, WhatsApp, discount, size upgrade, and urgency into one CTA block.
- Ad copy that promises a different outcome than the landing page.
