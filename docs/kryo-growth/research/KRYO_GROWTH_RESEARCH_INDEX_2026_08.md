## 6. Immediate experiment sequence

### Baseline

Deploy Control v2 high-confidence upgrades:

- truthful allocation scarcity;
- stronger product gallery;
- transparent tester/customer proof;
- 30-day trial + free Dubai delivery/returns at buy decision;
- subordinate WhatsApp specialist route;
- preserve one model, direct ATC, Downpay, free-upgrade popup and checkout.

### Creative discovery

Inside the existing winning Purchase ad set:

- keep historical winner;
- add one Morning Purpose pre-frame using the proven visual assets;
- point initially to Control v2;
- judge cost/ATC, LPV→ATC, checkout and mature purchase economics.

### Major validation test

Only if Morning Purpose demonstrates signal:

- create matched Morning first-20–25% landing-page treatment;
- then decide whether a controlled Meta Experiment is worth the budget cost.

### Next proof experiment

Real KRYO demonstration + authentic user proof package.

### Later assisted-conversion experiment

Targeted exit-intent → WhatsApp specialist, no extra discount by default.
