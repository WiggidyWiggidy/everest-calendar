## 1. Core strategic update

KRYO's conversion constraint is not only page layout. It is comprehension and belief.

A cold visitor has to quickly understand:

1. what KRYO is;
2. why it matters;
3. what it feels like;
4. why it is materially different from a normal cold shower;
5. why it is easier to use daily than a cold plunge;
6. what arrives in the box;
7. whether the product is real, premium and trustworthy;
8. why AED 3,990 is a rational purchase today.

A strong promotional/demo video can compress this cognitive work into 45-90 seconds. This is why the video is not merely a creative asset. It is a conversion asset, a proof asset and a sales-follow-up asset.

## 2. External research and benchmark notes

### Eight Sleep reference pattern

Eight Sleep does not sell only a piece of hardware. It sells a desired state: better sleep, better recovery and better daily performance.

Useful current reference points from 2026 research:

- Eight Sleep's Pod product pages prominently include a concise product explanation, risk reversal, free shipping/returns, warranty reassurance and a visible "See how the Pod works" video path.
- Eight Sleep's Pod 5 launch video frames the product as a complete immersive system, then explains the mechanism through product components and automated control.
- Eight Sleep's current page pattern is not "long page for the sake of length". It is early comprehension, early reassurance, then progressive proof.

KRYO should not copy Eight Sleep language. It should copy the information architecture:

> desired state → problem → product reveal → mechanism → proof of experience → life after use → risk reversal → action.

### Baymard product-video caveat

Product video helps explain products that static images cannot fully communicate, but it must supplement strong image/product proof rather than replace it. Video should be easy to find near the product/image/hero area and must not bury or distract from purchase controls.

### KRYO-specific interpretation

For KRYO, video is highest leverage because the product is new-category hardware. The buyer cannot already imagine the use moment.

The video must make the buyer think:

> "I understand it. I can see myself using it. It looks real. It looks premium. It will wake me up. It is much easier than a plunge."

## 3. KRYO promo video strategy

### Primary hypothesis

If KRYO visitors see a real, premium, founder/product-use video that shows the tired-to-switched-on transformation and proves the mechanism, then PDP ATC rate, checkout-start rate, returning-visitor quality and assisted sales conversion should improve.

### Value-equation pillar mapping

- **Dream outcome:** Step in tired. Step out switched on.
- **Perceived likelihood:** real product, real water, real temperature, real user reaction, founder/customer proof.
- **Time delay:** if operationally true, connect the first use to "tomorrow morning" or the next confirmed dispatch.
- **Effort/sacrifice:** existing bathroom, no tub, no ice, no dedicated space, no heavy maintenance ritual.

## 4. Recommended 60-90 second video structure

### Phase 1 — Problem / identity hook, 0-7 seconds

Visual:

- morning alarm;
- dim room;
- man half-awake;
- coffee not helping yet;
- Dubai heat / premium apartment context if natural.

Voiceover direction:

> Most people spend their first hour trying to wake up.

Do not over-explain. Make the viewer recognise the state.

### Phase 2 — Desired outcome, 7-15 seconds

Visual:

- slow body language shifting toward action;
- water / bathroom / morning routine setup.

Voiceover direction:

> KRYO was built for the moment before the day starts.

### Phase 3 — Product reveal, 15-25 seconds

Visual:

- clean product beauty shots;
- Halo detail;
- cooling unit;
- button press;
- cold water beginning.

Voiceover direction:

> Introducing KRYO. The cold immersion system built for sharper mornings.

Avoid: "cold shower upgrade", "shower attachment", "cold plunge alternative", "shower format".

### Phase 4 — Mechanism proof, 25-45 seconds

Visual:

- water flow from Halo;
- temperature display / cold water proof if visually clean;
- fill reservoir / complete-system context;
- product in actual bathroom.

Voiceover direction:

> 1°C cold immersion at the press of a button. Controlled water. Full upper-body coverage. Built into the bathroom routine you already have.

### Phase 5 — Real use / reaction, 45-60 seconds

Visual:

- actual user steps in;
- 30-60 seconds of use compressed;
- real reaction, breathing, cold shock, then alertness.

Voiceover direction:

> Step in tired. Step out switched on.

This must feel real, not AI-rendered or overproduced.

### Phase 6 — Life after, 60-75 seconds

Visual:

- laptop / work / walking outside / training / focused morning;
- premium, controlled, restrained.

Voiceover direction:

> Start the first 30 minutes with your body awake before the day demands it.

### Phase 7 — Offer / risk reversal, 75-90 seconds

Visual:

- product hero shot;
- what arrives;
- CTA frame.

Voiceover direction:

> Try KRYO for 30 mornings. If it does not switch you on, we collect it and refund you.

If operationally true:

> Dubai orders can be delivered next day.

If not operationally true:

> Limited August dispatch. Delivery date confirmed before checkout.

## 6. Page placement rule

First test should not be a full PDP rebuild.

Recommended clean test order:

1. current winning PDP + video directly below hero or immediately after first purchase opportunity;
2. measure PDP ATC, checkout-start, video engagement, page load and purchase CVR;
3. only then integrate video into a broader premium PDP rebuild.

Do not test new hero, new cart copy, new price and new video at the same time.

## 8. Research memory protocol

The strategy is evolving quickly through ChatGPT research, user observations, Shopify data, Supabase readouts, GitHub docs and future competitor research. Valuable insights can be lost if they remain only in chat.

### Rule

When a research conclusion changes how KRYO should prioritise, message, test, film, price, ship, follow up or measure, it must be captured in at least one of:

1. `public.marketing_findings` for research conclusions and evidence;

3. a GitHub research note for durable operating doctrine;
4. `marketing_change_log` when the change goes live.

Do not rely on memory or chat alone for anything that affects execution.

## 10. Conflict-resolution protocol

When new research contradicts old research, do not pick one automatically.

Resolve in this order:

1. **Live KRYO commerce data** beats all theory.
2. **Fresh first-party behavioural data** beats generic benchmarks.
3. **High-quality external research** beats anecdotal examples.
4. **Category-leader patterns** are useful, but must be translated to KRYO's stage, trust level and traffic source.
5. **User/founder insight** is high-value hypothesis input, not proof by itself.
6. **Old KRYO docs** remain historical unless refreshed or reconfirmed.

Every conflict should be logged as:

- what changed;
- what older belief it conflicts with;
- what evidence supports each side;
- what test or operating rule resolves it;
- whether the older finding is still active, partially superseded or archived.

## 11. Examples of current conflicts

### Conflict A — Video is high leverage vs users may skip video

Resolution:

Video is high leverage for KRYO because comprehension is a bottleneck, but it must supplement static images, not replace them. Preserve strong gallery and what-is-in-box assets.

### Conflict B — Cold plunge comparison helps value anchoring vs hurts category creation

Resolution:

Keep comparison, but reframe around daily friction and use. Do not lead with "cheaper cold plunge alternative". Use cold plunge as an anchor after KRYO's morning-performance outcome is established.

### Conflict C — WhatsApp helps high-ticket sales vs can cannibalise checkout

Resolution:

Use WhatsApp as a subordinate assisted-sales lane after product comprehension, not as a hero replacement or equal-weight CTA until data proves otherwise.

### Conflict D — Full PDP rebuild is high leverage vs current winner should be protected

Resolution:

Do not rebuild immediately. Test current PDP + video/proof first. Full PDP rebuild becomes high priority after assets exist and current readouts are protected.
