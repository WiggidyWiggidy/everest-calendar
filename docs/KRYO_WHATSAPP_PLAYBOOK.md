## Setup (10 min, once — recommended before July 13)
1. Install **WhatsApp Business** (can run alongside personal WhatsApp on the same phone with the same or a second number).
2. Greeting message (auto-sends on first contact):
   > Hi — you've reached Tom, founder of KRYO. I answer these personally, usually within a few hours. Ask me anything: delivery, installation, the guarantee, or whether it's actually as cold as we say. (It is.)
3. Away message (for China factory days):
   > I'm at our production facility this week, so replies can take a few hours. I'll come back to you personally — every message gets answered.
4. Labels: `new` → `talking` → `closed-won` / `closed-lost (reason)`. The reason is gold — log it.

## The rhythm of every conversation
1. **Answer their actual question first.** Straight, specific, no pitch.
2. **Offer the demo:** "Want a 2-minute video of the unit running? I can send it here." (When live video-call is possible: offer that — strongest trust move available.)
3. **Surface their objection** — don't guess: "What's the main thing you'd want to be sure of before going ahead?"
4. **Match the close-tool to the objection** (below). One tool per conversation — don't menu them.
5. **If not ready:** "No pressure — this batch dispatches August 30, 5 units total. Want me to hold a unit while you decide? AED 200, fully refundable, comes off the price."
6. **Before ending any conversation that didn't close today:** ask consent to follow up — see below. Without a yes, don't message them again first.

## Consent to follow up (required — WhatsApp also enforces this technically)
WhatsApp only lets you freely message someone within 24 hours of their last message to you. After that, unprompted follow-ups need a pre-approved template, and marketing messages need their opt-in regardless. So always ask, in-chat, before the conversation ends:
> "OK if I message you if a unit opens up, or when the next batch launches?"
- **Yes** → label them `followup-ok`, safe to reach out later.
- **No / no answer** → don't initiate again. They can always message you.
This costs nothing and keeps every follow-up clean.

## Objection answers (from the trust-gap analysis — keep these exact facts)
- **"Will it really get that cold?"** → "1–15°C, it chills its own 12L reservoir — doesn't depend on your building's water. I'll send the video."
- **"Is it safe in a bathroom?"** → "Only 48V DC enters the bathroom side — mains stays outside the wet area on a 5m cord. IPX4 splash-resistant, corrosion-resistant aluminium."
- **"What if it breaks / I don't like it?"** → "30 days, any reason: message me here, full refund, we arrange the return. The risk sits with us."
- **"Why no reviews?"** → "Because you'd be one of only 5 units in this Dubai batch. That's why you're talking direct instead of a support desk."
- **"When would I get it?"** → This batch dispatches August 30. *(Only promise dates you'll keep — a missed date to an early customer costs more than the sale.)*

## Rules
- Never invent specs, dates, or logistics not listed here. "Let me confirm and come back to you" beats a wrong answer.
- Log every closed-lost reason — that list becomes the page copy and the next ad.
- Target response: same day, always. Speed is the product here.
